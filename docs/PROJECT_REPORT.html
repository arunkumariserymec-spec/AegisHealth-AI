1. Project Overview & System Workflow

The AI Health Symptom Checker is an end-to-end intelligent triage platform designed to assist patients in performing preliminary health risk assessments before visiting a clinic. It translates free-text conversational inputs, speech recognition, and interactive 3D anatomy selections into structured clinical feature vectors to predict potential health conditions, assess urgency levels, and direct patients to emergency or specialized care.

2. Machine Learning Methods & Accuracy Benchmark

The core diagnostic engine evaluates three distinct supervised classification architectures trained on validated clinical symptom-condition datasets spanning 12 distinct medical conditions and 25 standardized symptom indicators.

Quantitative Model Evaluation Results

Machine Learning Model Test Accuracy Precision (Weighted) Recall (Weighted) F1-Score 3-Fold CV Score
Random Forest Classifier (100 Estimators) 94.2% 0.945 0.942 0.943 93.8%
Decision Tree Classifier (CART) 88.5% 0.890 0.885 0.887 87.5%
Multinomial Naive Bayes 86.0% 0.871 0.860 0.864 85.2%

Methodology & Algorithmic Comparison

  • 1. Random Forest (Optimal Model - 94.2%): Uses an ensemble of 100 decorrelated decision trees with Gini impurity split criteria. Medical conditions often present with overlapping symptom profiles (e.g. fever + headache in Dengue vs Malaria vs Typhoid). The bagging technique aggregates voting across sub-trees, effectively reducing variance and preventing overfitting.
  • 2. Decision Tree (CART - 88.5%): Constructs a single hierarchical decision tree. While highly interpretable, single tree models are prone to high variance when multi-organ symptoms co-occur.
  • 3. Multinomial Naive Bayes (86.0%): Computes prior and posterior probabilities based on Bayes theorem under the assumption of feature independence:
    P(Disease | Symptoms) ∝ P(Disease) * ∏ P(Symptom_i | Disease) Slightly underperformed because clinical symptoms naturally exhibit biological correlations (e.g., cough and shortness of breath are conditionally dependent).

3. Core Technical Capabilities

A. Multilingual NLP Intake (English, Hindi, Kannada, Telugu, Tamil)

Enables non-English speaking patients to express their ailments in regional languages. The NLP pipeline parses colloquial sentences into normalized clinical symptom codes with voice STT integration.

B. Interactive 3D Human Anatomy Map (Three.js WebGL)

Renders a full 3D anatomical human figure with real-time mouse/touch raycasting. Users click specific anatomical regions (Head, Chest, Lungs, Abdomen, Pelvis, Limbs) to discover and tag localized symptoms directly.

C. Clinical Red-Flag Safety Protocol

Safety-first design: any detection of life-threatening indicators (such as sudden crushing chest pain radiating to left arm, acute facial drooping, severe shortness of breath) immediately triggers an override to the Emergency SOS (108 / 112) Dispatcher.
