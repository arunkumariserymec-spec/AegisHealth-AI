1 Introduction & Regulatory Compliance

The AI Health Symptom Checker ("Application", "we", "us") is dedicated to protecting the privacy and sensitive medical inquiries of its users. This policy outlines how data is gathered, processed, and secured in compliance with:

  • Google Play Developer Health Apps Policy (Health & Sensitive User Data).
  • Apple App Store Review Guidelines (Section 5.1 Privacy and Data Use, Section 1.4 Medical Safety).
  • Information Technology (Reasonable Security Practices and Procedures and Sensitive Personal Data or Information) Rules.

2 Information We Collect

We strictly collect only the data necessary to provide preliminary clinical triage calculations:

A. Health Symptoms (Sensitive Personal Data)
Symptoms selected via the 3D Body Anatomy map or typed into the conversational check tool (e.g., fever, cough, chest pressure, joint pain, duration, severity ratings). This data is fed into the local Random Forest machine learning classifier to produce triage condition probabilities.
B. User Demographics (Optional)
Age and biological sex when optionally provided by the user, used strictly to calibrate age-specific clinical reference thresholds (e.g., pediatric vs. geriatric triage adjustments).
C. Account & Identification (Registered Users Only)
Email address and display name if you voluntarily choose to register an account. Guest users can utilize all symptom checker and 3D anatomy features without creating an account or providing an email address.

3 Purpose of Data Processing

We process collected data exclusively for the following legitimate purposes:

  • Preliminary Triage & Assessment: Evaluating symptom co-occurrence using ensemble machine learning models (Random Forest, Decision Tree, Naive Bayes).
  • Life-Threatening Emergency Detection: Identifying acute red-flag symptoms (such as cardiac arrest or stroke symptoms) and providing immediate one-tap calling to emergency dispatch (108 / 112 in India).
  • Care Navigation: Displaying verified local healthcare specialists and hospital facilities in Ballari, Bengaluru, and nearby regions.
  • No Advertising & No Data Selling: We do NOT sell, lease, or distribute your personal or health data to data brokers, advertisers, pharmaceutical corporations, or insurance providers.

4 Third-Party Sub-Processors & AI Processing

To enable conversational symptom understanding and secure cloud storage, we interact with the following secure sub-processors:

  • Google Gemini API / Cloud Run: Secure natural language processing for conversational symptom extraction. Prompt inputs are strictly ephemeral and not retained to train foundation models.
  • Firebase Firestore & Authentication: Encrypted data persistence (AES-256 at rest, TLS 1.3 in transit) for registered users.

5 User Rights & Account Deletion Mechanism

In accordance with Google Play and Apple App Store guidelines, users have complete authority over their data:

  • Access: Review your consultation logs at any time in the History section.
  • Local Erasure: Clear your browser storage to wipe all session data immediately.
  • Account & Data Deletion: You may request complete deletion of your account and all associated cloud records by emailing arunkumar.ise.rymec@gmail.com with the subject "Data Deletion Request". Requests are processed within 48 hours.
